Confetti — Multi-Color Pastel
Option B from v1 (peach + black + cream) is shown as reference. Three new palettes emphasise multi-color pastels as requested. The simulation below each palette approximates what confetti.js would look like at those colors against a cream background.
Option B-ref — v1 Pick (reference)
Peach, black, cream — editorial mono
SOLVED
Restrained, editorial. Matches black-and-cream base palette. Less celebratory, more refined.
Option C — Pastel Rainbow
6-color pastel spread, airy
SOLVED
Full spectrum pastels. Feels celebratory and soft — like a garden party. Works with any accent color.
Option D — Pastel Warm
Peach, coral, gold, blush — warm cluster
SOLVED
All warm tones — cohesive and cosy. Feels harmonious with peach/apricot/rose accent colors.
Option E — Pastel Cool
Lavender, sky, mint — cool cluster
SOLVED
Calm and airy. Works best if the accent color is also cool (lavender, sky). May feel muted against a warm accent.
