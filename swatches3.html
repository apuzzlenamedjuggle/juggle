§D — Round 3
Streak Badge
The constraint: it must sit comfortably to the right of the ? button in the header, communicate "days in a row" at a glance, and look like it belongs to the same visual language as the rest of the game. Eight metaphors — each shown at 3 streak lengths (3, 7, and 21 days) so you can see how the concept scales.
